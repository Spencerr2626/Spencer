## Recommendation

### Best Strategy: Buy & Hold — 1-bed unit, patient 7–10 year horizon

Verve Suites is a **yield + expat-tenant play**, not a capital appreciation or flip play. The property has genuinely strong tenant demand (proximity to GIS, MKIS, 1 Mont Kiara, SPRINT access, concierge facilities) and a liquid resale market. But:

- **Entry price must be right.** At median RM 866 psf, the yield is thin (~4.1% gross). Target at or below RM 800 psf for a 1-bed.
- **Hold past Year 6** (0% RPGT) — this is non-negotiable for any meaningful net return.
- **Monthly top-up ~RM 900–1,000** is required at 90% MOF — budget for this.
- **BRRRR** only works on a deeply distressed studio bought at RM 370–390K — rare but possible.
- **Flip is not viable** at current price levels. Thin spread, high density, RPGT drag.

| Buyer Profile | Recommendation |
|---------------|---------------|
| Investor, 1st/2nd property, RM 86K cash | Buy & Hold 1-bed — top up RM 935/mo, hold 7–10 yr |
| Investor, 3rd+ property, limited budget | Pause — 70% MOF makes cash-in very large (RM 185K) |
| Renovation enthusiast, distressed unit | BRRRR only if entry ≤ RM 390K — very specific deal |
| Short-hold flipper | **Pass** — RPGT + thin spread = losing trade |
| Expat-own-stay (MM2H, RM 1M threshold) | Minimum RM 1M for foreign buyers; Verve Suites qualifies |

### The Catch
**881 units across 4 towers = significant internal resale competition.** When you want to sell, you'll be competing with dozens of other units in the same project. This suppresses appreciation upside and makes it harder to command a premium. If exclusivity or strong capital appreciation is the goal, consider Hijauan Kiara (188 units, ≤2/floor) instead.
