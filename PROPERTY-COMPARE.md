## Recommendation

**Overall Winner: Mont Kiara Aman (72.7)** — by a whisker over Hijauan Kiara (72.2). Aman wins for the *investor* on yield, liquidity and lowest carry, while staying freehold in the MK core. But the two are effectively tied, and the right pick depends on the buyer's goal:

- **Best for Cash Flow / Yield:** **Mont Kiara Aman** — smaller units + low entry = best gross yield and easiest to let.
- **Best for Capital Appreciation / Own-Stay:** **Hijauan Kiara** — scarcity and ultra-low density preserve value and offer the nicest lived-in product.
- **Best for Own-Stay / First Luxury Home:** **Hijauan Kiara** (lifestyle) or **Mont Kiara Aman** (entry budget from RM 1.48m).
- **Best for Renovate-and-Resell:** **Mont Kiara Damai** — oldest, deepest psf discount, large units to add value (mind RPGT if selling before Year 6).
- **The Catch (on the winner, Aman):** it's the **densest** of the three (345 units across 2 towers), so it trades exclusivity for liquidity — and you'll compete with other owners on resale within the same project. If privacy/prestige matters more than yield, Hijauan is the better buy.
