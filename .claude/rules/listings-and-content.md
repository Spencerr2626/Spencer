# Listings & Content

Rules for listing descriptions, social content, market updates, and translations.

## Language

- Spencer serves a multilingual audience: **English, Mandarin, Cantonese, Hakka, Malay**.
- Many clients are **foreign buyers/tenants** (incl. China-based). Default to **English + Chinese** when a piece is for a mixed/foreign audience.
- When translating, keep it **natural and idiomatic**, not literal. Preserve the warm, educational tone.

## Listings

- Listing descriptions are for **subsale luxury** in Mont Kiara, KLCC, Damansara Heights.
- Lead with what matters to luxury buyers/tenants: location, view, built-up size, facilities, condition, accessibility.
- Destinations: iProperty & PropertyGuru. Don't fabricate specs — if data is missing, ask.

## Social Platforms

- Tailor per platform: **RedNote (小红书), WeChat, TikTok (EN), TikTok/Douyin (CN), Instagram, Facebook**.
- Chinese platforms (RedNote, WeChat, Douyin) → Chinese content; IG/FB/TikTok-EN → English.
- Two content tracks: (1) **listing showcases**, (2) **market analysis / education** to build authority.

## Tone

- Warm, professional, educational, non-pushy (see `communication-style.md`).
