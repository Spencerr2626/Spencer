# Relationship-Bank Campaign

Systematic re-connect with the **56 done-deal contacts** to generate referrals, repeat
business, and lease renewals, the warmest and cheapest leads Spencer has. Directly attacks
the listing/lead-supply bottleneck.

_Started: 2026-07-30 (Q3 focus). See `context/goals.md`._

## The bank, segmented

| Segment | Count | Why they matter | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Past tenants** | ~38 | Lease renewals = recurring commission; many will upgrade to buy; expats refer | Renewal, move, or referral |
| **Landlords / owners** | ~9 | **Listing supply** (re-let / sell / more units) + refer other owners | New listing or referral |
| **Past buyers / investors** | ~12 | Repeat investment; refer friends; market-update touch | Next buy or referral |
| **Referrers** | 7 (overlap) | Already trust Spencer, keep them warm | Thank + soft ask |

Language: ~half **Mandarin**, ~half **English** (+ a few Malay). Match each client.

## Execution (roll over ~2 weeks, ~10-15 personal messages/day)

- **Wave 1 (highest ROI):** Landlords (listings) + tenants **near lease renewal**.
- **Wave 2:** Past buyers/investors + the 7 referrers.
- **Wave 3:** Remaining tenants.
- Log every reply in the CRM. Anyone with a live need → move to **Active Leads**.
- Tone: warm, client-first, non-pushy (Spencer's brand). Personalise the [Name]/[Property].

## Templates

### A. Past tenants
**EN:** Hi [Name], hope you're settling in well at [Property]. Just checking in, how's everything with the unit? Whenever you need anything, a renewal, a move, or you know someone looking to rent or buy in Mont Kiara, I'm always happy to help. No rush, just here for you.

**中文:** [Name]您好！最近在[Property]住得还习惯吗？😊 只是问候一下，看看单位一切都好。将来若需要续约、换房，或身边有朋友想在满家乐租房或买房，随时找我，我很乐意帮忙。不急，只是想让您知道我一直都在。

### B. Landlords / owners  (priority, listing supply)
**EN:** Hi [Name], hope you're doing well. Just checking in on your [Property] and how the tenancy's going. If you're ever thinking of re-letting, selling, or adding to your portfolio, I'd be glad to help. And if you know other owners who'd like a hand with their units, I'm always happy to take care of them. Here whenever you need.

**中文:** [Name]您好！近来可好？想问候一下您[Property]的出租情况。将来若考虑重新出租、出售，或增添投资单位，我都很乐意协助。若身边有其他业主朋友需要帮忙打理单位，也欢迎介绍给我。随时为您服务。

### C. Past buyers / investors
**EN:** Hi [Name], hope you and the family are well. Just checking in on your [Property], happy to share a quick market update on how values in the area are trending if that's useful. And if you're ever considering another investment or know someone looking to buy in Mont Kiara, I'd love to help. Always here.

**中文:** [Name]您好！近来一切都好吗？想问候一下您[Property]的近况。若有需要，我可以为您提供该区最新的市场行情与价格走势。将来若考虑再投资，或身边有朋友想在满家乐置业，欢迎随时找我。我一直都在。

### D. Referrers
**EN:** Hi [Name], just wanted to say a genuine thank you again for thinking of me and sending [referral] my way, it really means a lot. If anyone else ever needs a hand buying, selling, or renting in Mont Kiara, I'll always take good care of them for you. Grateful to have you in my corner.

**中文:** [Name]您好！真心再次谢谢您一直以来的信任与介绍，对我意义重大。将来若有朋友需要在满家乐买卖或租房，我一定会好好照顾他们，不辜负您的推荐。非常感恩有您的支持！
