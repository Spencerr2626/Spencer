# Work / Business Details

## Business Model

- **Markets:** Subsale luxury — Mont Kiara (core), KLCC, Damansara Heights
- **Client mix:**
  - ~50% foreign **tenants**
  - ~30% foreign **buyers**
  - ~20% local **landlords**
- Demand-side focus: most value delivered to buyers/tenants. Multilingual service (EN, Mandarin, Cantonese, Hakka, Malay) is a key differentiator for foreign clients.

## CRM Recommendation (off Excel)

- **Near term:** Move the Excel file into **Notion** (free, flexible, works well from China, easy pipeline + contact + lead-status views) or a well-structured Google Sheet with filtered views by lead temperature (hot/warm/cold).
- **Later (when team forms):** Consider a real-estate CRM like **Follow Up Boss**. Powerful but pricier and weaker for WeChat-driven China clients — not worth it as a solo agent yet.
- **Recommendation:** Stay on a structured Notion/Sheet system until team-building begins.
