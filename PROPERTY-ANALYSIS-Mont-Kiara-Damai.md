## Executive Summary

Mont Kiara Damai Resort Condominium is a freehold luxury high-rise at Jalan Kiara 2, Mont Kiara — KL's premier expat enclave. Developed by Sunrise Berhad and completed in 2004, the 40-storey, 230-unit tower offers unusually large units (2,272–11,000 sq ft) at 6 units per floor, giving it a boutique density rare in the corridor. Current asking prices of RM 707–1,020 psf straddle fair value vs the luxury comp set, but the building's own historical transactions average RM 677 psf — reflecting an age and liquidity discount vs newer peers like 10 MK, 11 MK, and Seni Mont Kiara.

The property scores **68/100 (Grade B — Watch / Selective Buy)**. Its strongest dimensions are Neighborhood Quality (85/100) and Comparable Value (71/100), underpinned by KL's best international school cluster, a robust Japanese/Korean/European expat tenant pool, and freehold Sunrise pedigree. The drag comes from thin rental yields (net 1.6–2.9%) and a negative leveraged cash flow across all scenarios — this is a capital-appreciation play, not a yield investment. The asking price premium above the current median transacted price (RM 677 psf) is the single biggest risk.

**Recommendation:** Selective buy for the patient equity investor who can hold 7–10 years and negotiate entry to RM 750–850 psf. A full renovation budget (RM 150–170k) is warranted to maximise rentability and eventual resale. Avoid at asking prices above RM 900 psf for standard units — that band competes directly with better-maintained newer stock. Flipping is not viable at any asking price in the current range.
