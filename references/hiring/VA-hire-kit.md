# First Hire: VA / Admin Kit

Everything to recruit and onboard Spencer's first hire, a Virtual Assistant / Admin, to take
back-office work off his plate so he stays in front of clients and listings.

_Created 2026-07-30. See `context/team.md` and `context/goals.md` (Q3 focus)._

## Why now

Three signed deals (~RM130k) are closing, giving runway. Spencer's time is the constraint,
not leads-per-se. A VA directly attacks the follow-up + admin load so he can farm listings and
work the relationship bank.

## Role summary

Part-time to start (scale to full-time), remote or KL-based, handling admin for a solo
Mont Kiara luxury real-estate agent.

## Duties (hand off in this order)

1. **CRM upkeep** — log leads, viewings, deals, statuses into the Excel CRM per `.claude/rules/crm-rules.md` conventions (IDs, tags, DD/MM/YYYY).
2. **Follow-up scheduling** — maintain the Follow-Up Schedule, set reminders, keep the daily triage current.
3. **Document prep** — draft tenancy agreements, offer letters, payment vouchers from existing templates.
4. **Portal listings** — upload and refresh listings on iProperty / PropertyGuru.
5. **Scheduling** — coordinate viewings and appointments; manage the calendar.
6. _(Later)_ basic content scheduling + bilingual WhatsApp/WeChat drafting support.

## Ideal profile

- Organised, detail-oriented, reliable, discreet with client data.
- **Bilingual English + Mandarin** (big plus, half the pipeline is China/Chinese-speaking).
- Comfortable with Excel/Google Sheets, WhatsApp, WeChat.
- Real-estate admin experience a bonus, not required.

## Where to hire (2026, Malaysia + remote)

- **Local KL:** Hiredly, JobStreet, LinkedIn, Facebook groups (KL admin / real-estate assistant).
- **Remote/overseas (cheaper):** OnlineJobs.ph (Philippines), Upwork.
- **Referrals** from Spencer's own network (often the best hires).

## Cost guide

- KL part-time admin: ~RM 1,500-2,500/mo · full-time ~RM 2,500-3,500/mo.
- Philippines remote VA (full-time): ~USD 500-800/mo (~RM 2,300-3,700).
- **Recommend: start part-time (~RM 1,500-2,000) to test fit before committing full-time.**

## Interview questions

1. Walk me through how you'd keep a CRM of 60+ contacts organised and up to date.
2. How do you chase multiple follow-ups without letting any slip?
3. Give an example of juggling several deadlines or documents at once.
4. How comfortable are you with WhatsApp/WeChat, and messaging clients in English and Mandarin?
5. How do you handle confidential client information (IC numbers, contacts, deal figures)?
6. Any experience with property portals (iProperty / PropertyGuru) or tenancy paperwork?

## First 30 days (onboarding)

- **Week 1:** Learn the CRM (structure, tags, conventions). Shadow the daily follow-up.
- **Week 2:** Own CRM logging (Spencer reviews). Start scheduling follow-ups.
- **Week 3:** Draft documents from templates (TA, offer letter, voucher). Upload/refresh 1-2 listings.
- **Week 4:** Run the daily follow-up triage + reminders independently; Spencer spot-checks.

## Guardrails

- **NDA / confidentiality** on client data. Access to the CRM only; no forwarding client info.
- Start with a **1-month paid trial** before a longer commitment.
- Keep Spencer as the only client-facing voice at first; VA supports behind the scenes.
